# Synthetic NH3 validation report

Training pairs: 2565; chronological holdout rows: 1728.

Raw RMSE: 2.2183 ppm; calibrated RMSE: 0.7102 ppm.

Descriptive agreement limits: -1.2363 to 1.4999 ppm; bias 0.1318 ppm.

Limits are mean difference ± 1.96 sample SD, not confidence intervals or physical acceptance limits. Serial correlation, reference uncertainty and concentration-dependent variance limit interpretation.

Coefficients were reloaded from calibration.json before holdout application. Source SHA-256 and training dates accompany the artifact. No holdout rows train the fit. Raw and calibrated completeness use all holdout rows.

These synthetic results establish no gas selectivity, traceability, farm performance or emission rate. CH4/N2O in the commissioning bench are interface fixtures, not validated instruments.
